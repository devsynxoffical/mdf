## 📌 How to Use These Prompts
Each shot is an **end-to-end Text-to-Video prompt**. You do not need an image. Simply copy the prompt directly into:
1. **Google VideoFX (Google Veo)** (with your Google AI Pro account)
2. **Higgsfield Pro / Runway Gen-3 / Sora**

Each prompt includes:
- **Cinematography & Camera Movement** (Lens, axis, speed)
- **Subject & Action Detailing** (Exact micro-movements, lighting, specular highlights)
- **Environment & Atmosphere** (Color palette, volumetric depth, particles)
- **Negative Prompts & Rendering Parameters** (60fps, 4K, 16:9)

# 🚀 Quick Execution Guide for Google Veo (VideoFX) & Higgsfield

1. **Open Google VideoFX**:
   - Go to [labs.google/fx/tools/video-fx](https://labs.google/fx/tools/video-fx) with your **Google AI Pro** account.
2. **Copy & Paste**:
   - Select **Text to Video** and set **Aspect Ratio** to `16:9`.
   - Copy the **Text-to-Video Master Prompt** for each shot above.
3. **Generate 24 Clips**:
   - Each prompt generates a continuous 5-second cinematic clip.
4. **Final Assembly**:
   - Drop the 24 clips into CapCut Desktop or Premiere.
   - Add an ambient tech electronic synth track and export your final **2-Minute Showreel**!
