Why it usually fails

Why most home improvement lead generation disappoints

◎

Big projects, long decisions

Kitchens, baths, additions and exteriors are considered purchases. Homeowners collect several estimates over weeks, so a single call is rarely enough.

⏱

Budget mismatch

Without asking budget and scope up front, you spend evenings estimating projects the homeowner was never going to fund.

▦

Projects you do not want

Generic ads bring in small repair jobs when your crews are set up for full remodels. The offer has to match the project you want to sell.

FAQ

Home Improvement lead generation questions

What does a home improvement lead cost?+

In the receipt above, a Florida home-services company paid $11.27 per website lead over one week. Remodeling leads usually cost more than small exterior jobs because the projects are larger; we report your real number from your own ad account.

Do you work with remodelers and kitchen and bath companies?+

Yes. Campaigns are built per project type, so a kitchen and bath company and a siding contractor get different offers, creative and qualifying questions. Roofing is handled by our sister brand RoofingSystems.co.

Can you filter out small jobs and low budgets?+

Yes. The quote funnel asks project type, size and budget range, and routes leads below your minimum to a different follow-up instead of your estimator's calendar.

Can homeowners ask about financing?+

The funnel can ask whether they want financing, so your team knows before the visit and can bring the right options.
