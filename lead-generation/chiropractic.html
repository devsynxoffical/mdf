FAQ

Chiropractic lead generation questions

Does Facebook advertising work for chiropractors?+

It can, when the offer is specific and the follow-up is tight. In the receipt above, a US clinic sold 44 prepaid consults for $6,153 on $1,714 of ad spend in six days.

Should the first visit be free or paid?+

We usually recommend a small paid offer. People who pay up front are far more likely to attend, and the ads can pay for themselves.

Can you target only my local area?+

Yes. Ads are limited to the radius your patients come from.

Is the advertising compliant?+

We avoid medical claims Meta does not allow, and you approve every ad before it runs.
