FAQ

Solar lead generation questions

What is a good cost per solar lead on Meta Ads?+

It varies by state, offer and season. In the receipt above, a solar company paid $14.71 per lead across 300 leads in April 2026. We report your real number from your own ad account.

Are the solar leads exclusive?+

Yes. Ads run in your account, leads go to your CRM, and they are never shared or resold.

How do you filter out renters and low bills?+

The funnel asks qualifying questions such as homeowner status and average monthly bill, and routes non-qualified leads away from your calendar.

Can you book appointments for my closers?+

Yes. Follow-up and booking are automated, and we can add an AI appointment setter that replies and books by text.
