What a VSL funnel is

A VSL (video sales letter) funnel is a short sequence of pages built around a single sales video. Traffic from ads, email or content lands on the video page; the video explains the problem, the method and the offer; and a clear call to action sends interested viewers to the next step.

For a high-ticket offer that next step is usually an application or a booked call. For a lower-priced product it is a checkout with an order bump and an upsell — a low-ticket funnel. Either way, the funnel only works when the video, the page copy, the next step and the follow-up all say the same thing. New to the format? Start with our guide: what is a VSL?

What we build in a VSL funnel install

Every install covers the full path from first click to booked call or purchase:

  • ✓Offer and angle mapping before anything is designed, so the video sells one outcome to one audience
  • ✓VSL script structure: hook, problem, mechanism, proof, offer and call to action
  • ✓A mobile-first video page with the call to action timed to the pitch
  • ✓Application form or calendar booking for high-ticket offers, or checkout with order bump and upsell for low-ticket offers
  • ✓CRM pipeline, tags and stages so every viewer who opts in is tracked
  • ✓Email, SMS and reminder sequences for people who watched but did not act, and for booked calls
  • ✓Pixel, UTM and conversion tracking so you can see where viewers drop off

Who a VSL funnel is right for

VSL funnels work best when the buyer needs to understand something before they will buy — a coaching programme, a consulting engagement, a done-for-you service or a product with a new mechanism. If your best sales calls all start with you explaining the same thing, a VSL can do that explaining before the call. If your audience prefers a scheduled event, a webinar funnel does the same job live.

They are a poor fit when the offer is already understood and price-driven, where a short landing page usually converts better and costs less to produce.

How we measure a VSL funnel

We track the funnel step by step instead of judging it on one number: how many visitors press play, where viewers drop off in the video, how many reach the call to action, how many complete the application or checkout, and how many booked calls show up. Fixing the weakest step is almost always cheaper than buying more traffic.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a VSL be?+

As long as it takes to make the case for the offer and no longer. Short videos of a few minutes suit simple or low-priced offers; high-ticket offers often need longer videos because the viewer has more questions to answer before applying. We decide length from the offer, then use drop-off data to tighten it.

Do you write the VSL script?+

We map the offer and build the script structure with you, then shape the page and follow-up around it. You or your team record the video, because buyers respond to the person they will be working with.

Which platform do you build VSL funnels on?+

We build on the platform that fits your stack, most often GoHighLevel or ClickFunnels, and connect it to your CRM, calendar and payment processor.

How long does a VSL funnel take to launch?+

Most installs are live on real traffic in three to four weeks, depending on how clear the offer is and how quickly the video is recorded.
