Why it usually fails

Why most landing page design agency disappoints

◎

Homepages used as landing pages

Sending ad traffic to a homepage with ten links and no single action wastes most of the clicks you pay for.

⏱

Slow, heavy pages

On mobile, every extra second of load time loses visitors before they see the offer.

▦

No proof where doubt appears

Visitors need reasons to believe at the exact point they hesitate. Most pages put proof in the wrong place or not at all.

FAQ

Landing Page Design questions

How many landing pages do I need?+

Usually one per offer and traffic source, so the page matches the ad that brought the visitor. A single business often starts with one main page and adds variations once the first one converts.

What does a landing page design agency do?+

It plans, writes, designs and builds pages for a single offer and action, usually for paid traffic, and connects them to forms, calendars, CRM and tracking.

Which platforms do you build landing pages on?+

Usually GoHighLevel, and also ClickFunnels or your existing platform.

Do you write the copy?+

Yes. Copy, design, build, integrations and tracking are all included.

How is this different from a sales funnel?+

A landing page is one step. A funnel connects several steps, such as landing page, application, booking and follow-up. We build both.
