FAQ

Sales Funnels for Coaches questions

What is the best sales funnel for coaches?+

It depends on your price point and audience. Webinars and VSLs suit higher-ticket programs; low-ticket funnels work well for building a buyer list first. We recommend one after a short call.

Do you build funnels for consultants too?+

Yes. The same funnels work for consultants, course creators and other expert-led businesses.

Can you work with my course platform?+

Usually yes. We build in GoHighLevel by default and connect Kajabi, Teachable, Thinkific or Stripe where needed.

Do you have coaching results?+

Yes. Our coaching case study shows 13,630 low-ticket sales and $847,307 in revenue at a 3.32 ROAS.
