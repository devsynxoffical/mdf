A2P 10DLC Registration: What It Is and How to Get Approved

By the Million Dollar Funnel™ team·November 16, 2026·Updated November 26, 2026·9 min read
Short answerA2P 10DLC registration is the process of registering your business (the brand) and your texting use case (the campaign) with US mobile carriers through The Campaign Registry before sending business text messages from a standard 10-digit phone number. Unregistered business texts are filtered or blocked by carriers.

What A2P 10DLC means

A2P means application-to-person: messages sent by a business system, such as a CRM, to a person's phone. 10DLC means 10-digit long code: an ordinary local US phone number, as opposed to a short code or a toll-free number. A2P 10DLC is the US carriers' framework for business texting from those local numbers.

Under the framework, every business that texts customers from a 10-digit number must identify itself and describe how it uses SMS. Carriers use that information to decide how much traffic to allow and to filter spam.

Why registration matters

Since 2023, US carriers have blocked SMS sent through unregistered 10DLC numbers. For any business that relies on text follow-up, appointment reminders, lead nurture, no-show recovery or an AI appointment setter, an unregistered or rejected campaign means messages silently fail to arrive, which wrecks your speed to lead.

What you need before you register

For the brand

  • Legal business name exactly as it appears on your tax registration
  • Business tax ID (EIN for US businesses, or the equivalent for your country)
  • Business type, industry and registered address
  • A working website that matches the business name
  • An authorised contact's name, email and phone number

For the campaign

  • The use case, such as customer care, appointment reminders or marketing
  • A clear description of what messages you send and why
  • Sample messages that include your business name and opt-out language
  • A description of how people opt in to receive texts, with the exact wording they agree to
  • Opt-out handling (STOP) and help handling (HELP)
  • A privacy policy that states mobile numbers and opt-in data are not shared with third parties for marketing

The registration steps

  1. Register the brand through your SMS provider or CRM, which submits it to The Campaign Registry for verification.
  2. Wait for brand verification. Some brands are verified quickly; others need extra vetting.
  3. Register the campaign: use case, description, sample messages, opt-in and opt-out details.
  4. Wait for carrier review of the campaign. This can take from a few days to several weeks.
  5. Attach your phone numbers to the approved campaign and send a test message.

What is A2P verification?

A2P verification is the review your brand and campaign go through before carriers accept your texts. The registry checks that your business details are real and match each other, and carriers check that your use case, opt-in and sample messages follow their rules. Once both are approved, your numbers can send at the volume your registration allows.

Common reasons campaigns are rejected

  • The business name, tax ID and website do not match each other
  • The website is missing, unfinished or does not describe the business
  • The opt-in process is unclear or does not show consent language
  • The privacy policy does not cover SMS data sharing
  • Sample messages do not include the business name or opt-out instructions
  • The use case selected does not match the sample messages

Most rejections come from mismatched or incomplete information rather than from the business itself. Fix the specific reason given, update the website or forms if needed, and resubmit.

Do you need 10DLC registration for a lead generation funnel?

Yes, if the funnel texts leads in the US from a local number. Instant text replies, reminders and missed call text back all count as business texting. Register before launch, because a lead generation funnel that cannot text loses most of its speed advantage.

A2P registration in GoHighLevel and other CRMs

If you text through a CRM such as GoHighLevel, registration is done inside that platform's phone settings, which pass your details to the carrier registry. The same requirements apply: matching business details, a compliant website and opt-in form, a privacy policy with SMS language, and sample messages with your name and opt-out instructions. Build the opt-in form and privacy policy before you submit, so the reviewer can see them. Our GoHighLevel team handles this as part of every install that uses SMS.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I send texts without A2P 10DLC registration?+

US carriers filter or block business texts from unregistered 10-digit numbers, so most messages never arrive and your sending number can be flagged.

What is the difference between brand and campaign registration?+

Brand registration identifies your business: legal name, tax ID, address and website. Campaign registration describes how you text: the use case, sample messages, opt-in and opt-out.

Do I need A2P 10DLC for missed call text back?+

Yes. Missed call text back sends business texts from a 10-digit number, so the number must be attached to an approved A2P 10DLC campaign.

Is A2P 10DLC registration required?+

Yes, for businesses sending text messages to US numbers from standard 10-digit numbers. US carriers block SMS from unregistered 10DLC numbers.

How long does A2P 10DLC approval take?+

Brand verification can be quick, but campaign review commonly takes from a few days to several weeks, longer if the campaign is rejected and resubmitted.

Can I register as a sole proprietor?+

Yes. Sole proprietor registration exists for businesses without a tax ID, but it has lower sending limits and fewer numbers than a standard registration.
