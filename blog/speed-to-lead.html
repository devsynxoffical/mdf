Speed to Lead: Why the First Minutes Decide the Sale

By the Million Dollar Funnel™ team·November 16, 2026·7 min read
Short answerSpeed to lead is the time between a lead's enquiry and your first response. The faster you respond, the more likely you are to reach and qualify the lead; research in Harvard Business Review found that contacting leads within an hour made companies nearly seven times as likely to qualify them.

What speed to lead means

Every lead form, booking request or missed call starts a clock. Speed to lead measures how long it takes your business to make its first meaningful contact: a call, a text or a reply that moves the conversation forward. An automated "thanks, we'll be in touch" email does not count.

What the research shows

A study published in Harvard Business Review in 2011, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads", analysed how thousands of US companies responded to web leads. Companies that tried to contact a lead within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify it as companies that waited even an hour longer, and far more likely than those that waited a day. Many companies took much longer than a day to respond at all.

The reason is simple: when someone fills in a form, they are thinking about the problem right now. Minutes later they are back at work, talking to a competitor, or have forgotten they asked.

Why most businesses respond slowly

  • Leads arrive outside office hours, at night and at weekends
  • Leads land in an inbox or spreadsheet instead of a CRM that alerts someone
  • Sales staff are on calls or delivering work when the lead comes in
  • Nobody owns follow-up, so each person assumes someone else replied
  • Missed calls are never called back

How to improve speed to lead

  1. Measure it: record the time of each enquiry and the time of first contact in your CRM.
  2. Send every lead source into one CRM so nothing waits in an inbox.
  3. Trigger an instant SMS that starts a real conversation, not just an acknowledgement.
  4. Use missed-call text back so a call you could not answer becomes a text conversation within seconds.
  5. Alert the right person immediately and set a target response time.
  6. Use an AI appointment setter to qualify and book leads that arrive when nobody is available.
  7. Follow up several times over the following days: many leads respond on the second or third attempt.

Missed call text back

Missed-call text back sends an automatic SMS to anyone whose call goes unanswered, for example: "Sorry we missed your call — this is (your business). How can we help?" It turns a lost call into a conversation that you or an assistant can continue by text. In the US, the number sending the text needs to be registered for A2P 10DLC so the message is delivered.

Speed alone is not enough

A fast reply that says nothing useful still loses the lead. The first message should acknowledge what the person asked for, ask one easy question that moves toward qualification, and make the next step obvious — usually booking a time to talk. Frequently asked questions

What is a good speed to lead?+

Aim to make meaningful contact within five minutes, and never more than an hour. Automation can respond in seconds, around the clock.

How do you measure speed to lead?+

Subtract the time a lead was created from the time of the first real contact attempt, and track the average and the slowest cases in your CRM.

Does AI help with speed to lead?+

Yes. An AI appointment setter can text or call new leads within a minute, qualify them and book calls, so speed no longer depends on who is free.
