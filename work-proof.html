What does a lead cost on Meta Ads?

Across the 14 receipts on this page that state a cost per lead, Meta Ads leads cost between $4.34 and $26.93, with a median of $13.50. The niche and the offer drove most of the difference:

  • Webinar registrations were the cheapest leads: $4.34–$5.27 across 3 receipts. A free or low-cost registration is a small ask.
  • Mortgage, solar, wealth and home-service leads ranged from $8.06 to $18.92 (7 receipts).
  • Personal-injury legal leads were the most expensive: $18.69–$26.93 (2 receipts), reflecting how competitive and valuable each case is.
